# About Me

I am **Luo Zhou (周骆)**, a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/), working with [Prof. Qian Zhang](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/about/people/faculty/qian-zhang).

I received my Ph.D. in Software Engineering from Donghua University, where I was advised by [Prof. Shan Chang](https://www.dhu.edu.cn/2021/0603/c19081a227424/pagem.htm). My research interests lie in mobile and wearable computing, IoT security and privacy, and ubiquitous sensing, with a particular focus on earable sensing, user authentication, and privacy-preserving intelligent systems.

<span class='anchor' id='research'></span>

# 🔬 Research Interests

- **Mobile and wearable computing:** sensing and interaction systems built around mobile, earable, and wearable devices.
- **IoT security and privacy:** authentication, side-channel threats, and privacy protection for intelligent devices.
- **Ubiquitous sensing:** practical sensing systems that connect physical signals, embedded devices, and real-world applications.
